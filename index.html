Clinical caveats (avoid misinterpretation)
• This is an estimate, not a replacement for indirect calorimetry.
• Use only with stable ventilation (no recent settings changes).
• Avoid with major air leaks, ECMO, or very unstable ventilation.
• Large dead space/V̇/Q̇ mismatch can reduce accuracy (PaCO₂ may not reflect alveolar CO₂).
• Prefer serial trends over single-point absolute targets.
• If RQ is unknown, default 0.85 is reasonable for mixed substrate use in ICU.
Equations used
VCO₂ (mL/min) = (VE × PaCO₂ / 863) × 1000
EE (kcal/day) = 1.44 × [3.941 × VO₂ + 1.106 × VCO₂], with VO₂ ≈ VCO₂/RQ (Weir)
Key references

Stapel SN et al. Crit Care. 2015;19:370. (VCO₂-derived EE concept)
